# Code of Conduct

Lorestra is a collaborative open-source project. Participants must be
respectful, assume good intent, discuss evidence rather than people, and make
space for different levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
and sharing private information are not acceptable.

Report a concern privately to the maintainers listed in the repository security
policy. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when
needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
