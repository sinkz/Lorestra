# Cygnus: repeatable evidence

> Fictional demonstration. These examples are not records of real incidents, learners, or research results.

This fictional desk-light study asks whether two observation sessions can be compared fairly. Its purpose is to demonstrate provenance and reproducibility, not to claim a scientific result or provide a calibrated measurement standard.

A useful record states the question, instrument, unit, conditions, and transformation applied to each value. Keep the raw observation separate from the derived table so another person can repeat the steps and locate a disagreement.

- Declare the unit before collection.
- Retain the original values and mark corrections as new records.
- Compare sessions only after checking their conditions and method.
