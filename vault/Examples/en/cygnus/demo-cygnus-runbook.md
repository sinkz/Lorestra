# Cygnus: collection protocol

> Fictional demonstration. These examples are not records of real incidents, learners, or research results.

This example protocol is shared with the Orion latency notebook because both need declared units and comparable observation windows. It describes record keeping, not laboratory safety, calibration, or a claim that two instruments are interchangeable.

1. State the question and identify the observation session.
2. Record the instrument label, unit, conditions, and sampling interval.
3. Preserve each raw value, including missing readings and annotations.
4. Create derived values with an explicit conversion or aggregation rule.
5. Ask a second person to reproduce one derived row from its sources.

Publish the method and its limitations together. Reopen the decision when conditions change instead of silently combining incompatible sessions.
