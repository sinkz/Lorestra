# Lyra: answer-only activity

> Fictional demonstration. These examples are not records of real incidents, learners, or research results.

This archived example asked only for a final interpretation and rewarded a complete-looking paragraph. It left too little evidence to distinguish independent reasoning, useful peer support, and copied conclusions.

Do not reuse the activity unchanged. It remains in the vault as context for the revised lesson and the copied-conclusions incident, showing why the process now includes an individual draft and an explanation of the revision.
