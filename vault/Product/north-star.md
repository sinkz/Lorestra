# Product north star

Lorestra exists to make useful context easy to find, trust, and carry forward. The product measures knowledge leverage, not document volume. A reader should reach the smallest useful context quickly, understand why it is trustworthy, and see what changed since the last time.

A contributor should be able to leave a proposal that another person can review without reconstructing the entire conversation. A team should be able to export the vault without losing the meaning of its documents, relations, and history.

## Product principles

- **Context over collection.** A short, connected document beats a warehouse of unowned notes.
- **Trust over fluency.** Provenance, version, owner, and review state are visible.
- **Portable by default.** Markdown remains useful without the application.
- **Human accountability.** Agents accelerate discovery; authorized people own publication decisions.
- **Calm surfaces.** Reading and reviewing should reduce cognitive load instead of adding dashboard noise.

The initial release favors a calm reading experience, transparent history, and a vault that can leave the application without losing meaning.
