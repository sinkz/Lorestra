# Research signals

Recurring signals from early discovery:

- Readers need a fast route from a familiar question to the relevant decision.
- Contributors fear publishing an unreviewed half-truth.
- Teams lose confidence when history is hidden.
- Agents need stable identifiers and bounded retrieval.
- A portable source matters when the application or storage provider changes.

These are inputs, not conclusions. Each signal remains linked to a research note and should be tested before becoming a roadmap promise. A proposal may quote the signal, but it should not present a small sample as universal demand.
