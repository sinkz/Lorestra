# Collaboration protocol

Every task starts with an owner, a desired outcome, the seam it may change, and a verification path. Parallel work is welcome when file ownership and interfaces are clear. Reviewers look for evidence and locality, not just activity.

## Working agreement

- Read the current plan and files before acting.
- State assumptions when a decision could change the product or security posture.
- Keep changes inside the requested seam and preserve unrelated user work.
- Record durable decisions in the vault; keep short-lived coordination in chat.
- Ask for review when a change crosses contracts, adapters, or publication state.
- End a task with a concise handoff: what changed, what remains, and what should happen next.

The protocol applies to humans and agents. An agent can accelerate research and implementation, but a person remains responsible for integrating work and granting publication authority unless a future policy explicitly says otherwise.
