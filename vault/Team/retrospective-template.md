# Retrospective template

Use this template after a milestone, incident, or collaboration experiment. Keep observations separate from interpretations and leave one owner for each action.

## What happened?

Name the period, scope, and evidence. Link the relevant proposal, revision, or external source.

## What helped?

Describe practices worth repeating and why they worked in this context.

## What hurt?

Describe friction, ambiguity, or risk without assigning blame. State what evidence supports the observation.

## What will change?

Create a small proposal for a durable process or document change. Record owner, success signal, and revisit date. A retrospective is not complete when the conversation ends; it is complete when the useful learning has a safe path to publication.
